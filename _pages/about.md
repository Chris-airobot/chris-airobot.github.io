I am **Tianyuan (Chris) Liu**, a PhD candidate at **Deakin University**, working with Prof. Richard Dazeley and Dr. Akansel Cosgun.

My research focuses on **robot learning, manipulation, reinforcement learning, and real-world execution**. I develop learning-based approaches that improve the reliability of physical manipulation, from evaluating grasp-conditioned placement candidates to contact-rich book insertion.

I have worked with **Franka Panda**, **xArm7**, and **Unitree G1**, combining simulation, learning and hardware experimentation. At Samsung Research, I contributed to perception, localization, calibration, and deployment integration for a team-developed humanoid manipulation system.
