**ACRA 2025 · Oral Presentation** · [arXiv Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18922)

**Authors:** Tianyuan Liu, Richard Dazeley, Benjamin Champion, Akan Cosgun.

This work learns geometric features for predicting inverse-kinematics and collision feasibility under grasp-conditioned target placements. The trained feasibility scores support budgeted robot placement selection.

**Platform:** Franka Panda.
